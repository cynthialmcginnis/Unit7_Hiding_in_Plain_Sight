# Hiding in Plain Sight

### ARIN 310, Unit 7: Applications of AI

> "As soon as it works, no one calls it AI anymore."
>
> John McCarthy, who coined the term "artificial intelligence"

You've already used AI today. It picked the shows on your streaming app, cleaned the spam out of your inbox, and estimated when you'd get where you were going. Most of it didn't look like AI at all.

These two flipbooks take you behind the screen: what AI really is, how it learns, and how one of the largest banks in the country put it to work.

**Nothing to install. No login. Open a link and start turning pages.**

## What you'll do in Hiding in Plain Sight

- Write your own definition of AI, with no textbook allowed, and test it against real systems
- Flip cards, fill in blanks, sort systems, and run a working recommender
- Watch a language model pick its next word
- Match six real applications to the AI they run on
- Come back to your first definition at the end and see how much it changed

## 🖨️ Keep your work

Your answers save in your browser as you go. When you finish:

1. Click **Print** at the top of the book.
2. Choose **Print my answers** for a short summary, or **Print the whole book with my answers** for every page.
3. Pick **Save as PDF** in the print window if you want a file instead of paper.

A few things to know:

- Your answers live on the device and browser where you typed them, so print from that same device.
- A private or incognito window may not keep your answers.
- On a shared computer, open the **Save your answers** page and click **Clear all my answers** before you leave.
